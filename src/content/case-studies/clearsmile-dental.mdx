## The Challenge

ClearSmile Dental operates three locations across the Dallas metro area. Like most dental practices, they faced a persistent revenue leakage problem: cancellations.

With 40–60 appointments per day across all locations, even a 12% cancellation rate meant 5–7 empty chair-hours daily. At an average production rate of $250/hour, that's $1,250–$1,750 per day — or up to $30,000/month — in lost revenue.

Their second problem: recall. Their database had 4,200 patients overdue for their 6-month cleaning, with no systematic way to contact them. The front desk was too busy managing the phones to run recall campaigns.

Third: appointment reminders. Their reminder system was a single automated email, which generated a 60% no-show rate on the days it was sent (too early, too generic).

## The Solution

### System 1: Real-Time Cancellation Fill

We built an automated cancellation response system:

1. When an appointment is cancelled in their practice management software (Dentrix), the system immediately identifies the gap
2. The AI pulls the waitlist for that location and time preference
3. Text and voice messages go to the top 5 waitlist patients simultaneously: *"A [date] at [time] appointment just opened at ClearSmile [location]. Reply YES to claim it, or call us at [number]."*
4. First to respond gets the slot; the system books automatically and sends confirmation
5. If no waitlist match within 20 minutes, the system searches for patients overdue for their cleaning who match the appointment type

**Result:** 94% of cancellation slots are filled within 45 minutes.

### System 2: Recall Campaign Engine

For the 4,200 overdue patients, we built a multi-channel recall campaign:

- **Week 1:** Personalized text message with easy booking link
- **Week 2:** Personalized voice message from "ClearSmile team" 
- **Week 3:** Email with a gentle reminder
- **Week 6:** Final outreach with a small incentive (free whitening tray)

All messaging is HIPAA-compliant and includes proper opt-out mechanisms.

**Result:** 1,100 of the 4,200 overdue patients booked within 90 days of launch.

### System 3: Smart Appointment Reminders

We replaced their single-email system with a multi-touch reminder sequence:

- **5 days before:** Email with appointment details and pre-visit instructions
- **2 days before:** SMS with one-tap confirm
- **Morning of:** Final reminder with parking/directions info

Patients can reply to reschedule. The system automatically opens the slot and texts the next waitlist patient.

**Result:** No-show rate dropped from 60% to 21%.
